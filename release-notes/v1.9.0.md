## Iran Censorship Monitor v1.9.0

v1.9.0 turns the dashboard into a plain-language answer, in English and Farsi, to what people in Iran can reach online. Every finding rests on measurements taken inside networks registered in Iran, is dated and names its source; outside-in sources remain context only. The technical measurements stay available in their own view.

### Overview for everyone

- a headline with what changed against the period before, what this means for you and what we do not know;
- the six main services with website, app and app-server results, "blocked since" (monthly history since January 2022) and the Iranian networks where a service was not blocked;
- ways around the filter: OONI's tests of Tor, Snowflake, Psiphon, Riseup VPN, STUN and encrypted DNS, with Tor, Psiphon Conduit and Cloudflare WARP usage from Iran;
- further services in five groups, including 19 AI services, with a per-network access table ("who has access");
- who decides what is blocked and privileged access, from dated primary sources only;
- the connection: outage traffic, how a nationwide shutdown unfolded hour by hour, connection quality;
- one design language, readable type (Vazirmatn for Persian), right-to-left Farsi throughout.

### Reports, pages and open data

- daily updates (`/updates`, `/feed.xml`, optional Telegram), weekly (Saturday to Friday) and monthly reports (`/reports`), printable as PDF;
- `/tools`: the ways around the filter measured from inside Iran, with devices and official download channels; `/sources`: every source with where it measures from and how it answered;
- open daily data for all of Iran (`/data/latest.json`, one file per day), documented in `docs/API.md`; embeddable status image (`/widget.svg`); CSV, PDF and Word export.

### Evidence and reliability

- claim-based interpretation: connectivity, interference, routing, quality and shutdown judged separately, with independent severity, confidence and coverage;
- OONI tests filed under Iran from networks registered abroad are left out and counted;
- a network without tests is answered from all of Iran, labelled; a source that fails or rate-limits shows its last good answer with its date;
- optional collector with a local store, and optional independent checks from RIPE Atlas and Globalping probes, limited to services whose use is not punishable in Iran;
- last good answers stored one file each, large answers compressed off the main thread, a cached Overview served from memory; offline copy of the last answer; `/api/health` for operators.

### Verification

- `npm run check`: syntax checks and all deterministic tests pass, including checks that versions, the test count and links in the documents are current;
- `npm run verify:release-notes`, `npm run build` and the live public-source acceptance (`npm run verify:public`) pass;
- headless browser checks of the Overview, Technical analysis, reports, tools and sources pages in English and Farsi, at desktop and phone width: no script errors, no untranslated text, no horizontal overflow;
- details and counts: `docs/VERIFICATION.md`.

### Deployment boundary

This release does not authorize an owned probe fleet in Iran; `deploymentAuthorized:false` remains. Active checks from RIPE Atlas and Globalping stay off unless an operator switches them on, and never target news, opposition or circumvention sites. Before hosting the dashboard publicly, work through `GO_LIVE.md`.
